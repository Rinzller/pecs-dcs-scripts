## VF-143 Pukin Dogs Air Threat Generator

A plug-and-play air threat generator that adds an F10 radio menu for spawning hostile aircraft during a mission.

### Features

* Easy, Medium, Hard, and Nightmare difficulty levels
* Random fighter and bomber packages
* Hot aspect spawns approximately 100 NM away
* 1 NM tactical line-abreast formations
* Veteran AI
* Built-in air-to-air loadouts
* Cleanup option
* No templates, MIST, or MOOSE required

## Installation

1. Open your mission in the DCS Mission Editor.
2. Open **Triggers**.
3. Create a new trigger:
   * **Type:** `MISSION START`
   * **Action:** `DO SCRIPT FILE`
4. Select `VF-143_Pukin_Dogs_Air_Threat_Generator.lua`.
5. Save the mission.

The script will automatically create the **F10 → Other → VF-143 Air Threat Generator** menu.
